Our Founder

Meet Jason Myburgh

Jason's love for animals started long before Paws Unlimited ever existed. Growing up in the Western Cape, he was the kid who rescued stray cats, volunteered at local shelters on weekends, and always had a dog at his side.

After more than a decade building a career in the corporate world, Jason made a decision that changed everything — he chose to turn his lifelong passion into his profession. He founded Paws Unlimited to give Cape Town pet owners genuine peace of mind when they're away.

Today, Jason is a certified professional pet sitter through WePetSit South Africa, trained in pet first aid, and has personally cared for hundreds of animals across the Cape Town Metro area. His approach is simple: treat every pet as if it were his own.

"When I'm with your pets, they're not a job — they're family."

FAQ

Questions we get all the time

If your question isn't answered here, just pop us a WhatsApp — we're always happy to chat.

Do you look after cats as well as dogs?

Absolutely. We care for dogs, cats, and most other household pets. Just let us know what fur (or feathered!) friends you have and we'll make sure they're comfortable.

What does a visit include?

Every visit includes feeding, fresh water, playtime or a walk, a general wellness check, and a WhatsApp update so you know your pet is happy and safe.

What if my pet has medication or special needs?

No problem at all. We're trained to administer oral medication and can accommodate most dietary or care requirements. Just let us know during the booking consultation.

How do I get started?

It's simple — send us a WhatsApp with your dates and pet details. We'll arrange a free meet-and-greet so your pet can get comfortable with us before the booking begins.

Are you insured?

Yes. Paws Unlimited operates within the WePetSit South Africa network, which upholds professional standards including appropriate insurance coverage for pet care services.

Do I need to provide food for the sitter?

Not at all. For overnight and live-in services, we bring everything we need and cater for ourselves entirely. You only need to provide your pet's food and supplies.
